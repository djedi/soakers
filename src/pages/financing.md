        <h2>Plan the Whole Project Before You Apply</h2>
        <p>The most common financing mistake is borrowing for the spa and forgetting everything around it. Before you apply, add up the full project so your monthly payment covers it all:</p>
        <ul class="guide-list">
          <li><strong>The spa itself.</strong> Prices vary by size, features and brand. Our <a href="/guides/hot-tub-cost-utah/">Utah hot tub cost guide</a> lists typical ranges, and we'll give you a current quote on the exact model.</li>
          <li><strong>Electrical.</strong> Full-size spas need a dedicated 240V circuit. Plug-and-play models like <a href="/spa-models/garden-spas/">Garden Spas</a> and the <a href="/spa-models/nordic-all-in-110v/">Nordic ALL-IN-110V</a> use a standard outlet. See <a href="/guides/110v-vs-240v-hot-tubs/">110V vs 240V</a>.</li>
          <li><strong>A level base.</strong> A concrete pad, compacted gravel or an engineered deck.</li>
          <li><strong>Accessories.</strong> <a href="/steps/">Steps</a>, a <a href="/covers/">cover lifter</a>, or a <a href="/covana/">Covana</a> automated cover, plus your first water-care supplies.</li>
        </ul>
        <h2>Questions to Ask Before You Sign</h2>
        <p>Rates, terms and approval come from Mountain America Credit Union, not from us. When you apply, it's worth asking:</p>
        <ul class="guide-list">
          <li>What is the APR, and is it fixed for the whole loan?</li>
          <li>How long is the term, and what is the monthly payment on the exact amount I'm borrowing?</li>
          <li>Is there any penalty for paying the loan off early?</li>
          <li>Can the loan cover accessories or installation work, or only the spa?</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Compare the total you'll pay over the life of the loan, not just the monthly payment. A longer term lowers the payment but usually raises the total cost.</p>
        <h2>Why Buy Local in Midvale</h2>
        <p>Financing a spa from a family-owned local dealer means the people who sold it to you are the people who deliver it, set it up, and service it afterward. We repair every brand, stock parts and chemicals in the showroom, and test water samples for free. Come see the models in person at 6905 S State St, Suite A, Monday through Saturday from 10 AM to 5 PM, or <a href="/contact/">request a quote</a> first so you know what to apply for.</p>
