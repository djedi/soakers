The honest answer to "how much is a hot tub?" is that the spa itself is only part of the bill. In Utah you should budget for three things: **the hot tub**, **getting it installed** (power, a level base, and delivery), and **running it** every month. This guide walks through each one with real ranges so you can plan before you shop.

## 1. The hot tub itself

Prices depend mostly on size, jet count and pumps, insulation, and the features you add (lighting, audio, water features, upgraded covers). These are the ranges we give shoppers in our Midvale showroom:

| Tier | Typical price | What you usually get |
|---|---|---|
| Entry-level | about $4,000–$6,000 | Smaller spas, simpler jet systems, often plug-and-play 120V |
| Mid-range | about $7,000–$12,000 | 5–7 seats, more jets and pumps, better insulation and features |
| Premium | about $12,000–$20,000+ | Advanced hydrotherapy, upgraded controls, lighting and audio, longer warranties |

A few examples from the lines we carry:

- **Compact and plug-and-play:** [Garden Spas](/spa-models/garden-spas/) and the [Nordic ALL-IN-110V Series](/spa-models/nordic-all-in-110v/) plug into a standard outlet, which keeps the total project cost down.
- **Value full-size spas:** [Nordic Luxury](/spa-models/nordic-luxury/), [Sport](/spa-models/nordic-sport/), [Modern](/spa-models/nordic-modern/) and [Classic](/spa-models/nordic-classic/) series, and the [South Seas Spas](/spa-models/south-seas-spas/) Standard and Deluxe classes.
- **Configurable to premium:** [Island Spas](/spa-models/island-spas/) come in Essential, Luxury and Elite trims, and the [Artesian Elite](/spa-models/artesian-elite/) line sits at the top with features like the DIRECTFLOW Personal Control System and a 5.5 kW titanium heater.

Prices change with model years and options, so ask us for a current quote on the exact spa and options you want.

## 2. Installation costs

Installation is where budgets most often go wrong. The figures below are **national averages from HomeGuide's 2025 hot tub installation cost guide**. Utah prices vary by contractor and by your yard, so treat them as a planning range, not a quote.

| Item | Typical range (national) | Notes for Utah homes |
|---|---|---|
| New 240V circuit, outlet/subpanel, up to 100 ft of wire | $800–$1,600 | Needed for most full-size spas. Skip it with a plug-and-play 120V model. |
| Electrical panel upgrade | $1,400–$2,800 | Older homes with 100- or 150-amp service may need one. |
| Concrete pad | $500–$2,500 ($5–$10 per sq ft) | The most durable base for a spa that will sit through freeze-thaw winters. |
| Gravel base | $1–$3 per sq ft | Cheaper, but it must be compacted and level. |
| Delivery and placement | $200–$500 | Gates, slopes and side-yard width matter. |
| Crane (if the spa can't go around the house) | $300–$1,500 | Only needed for a small share of installs. |
| Permit and inspection | $100–$250 | Electrical work usually needs a permit. Check with your city. |
| Steps and cover lifter | $60–$400 and $150–$400 | Steps matter more on snowy, icy evenings. |

**What Soakers handles:** we deliver, place, level, fill and start up the spas we sell, and we'll tell you up front exactly what electrical service your model needs. You'll want a licensed electrician for any new 240V circuit.

### Ways to keep installation costs down

1. **Choose a plug-and-play model** if your space and soaking habits fit one. [Garden Spas](/spa-models/garden-spas/) plug into a standard 120V/15A outlet with the included GFCI cord, and so do Nordic's [ALL-IN-110V](/spa-models/nordic-all-in-110v/) models.
2. **Put the spa close to your electrical panel.** Every extra foot of trench and wire adds cost.
3. **Use an existing patio** if it's level, solid and drains away from the house.
4. **Plan access before delivery day.** Measure gates and paths. A spa that can roll around the house avoids a crane fee.

For a deeper look at the electrical choice, read [110V vs 240V hot tubs](/guides/110v-vs-240v-hot-tubs/).

## 3. Monthly running costs

Most modern hot tubs cost roughly **$20–$50 a month in electricity in Utah**. Where you land in that range depends on:

- **Insulation.** Full-foam and other high-coverage insulation packages hold heat better.
- **Your cover.** A tight, dry, well-fitting [cover](/covers/) is the biggest single factor. Waterlogged covers waste heat.
- **The season.** Utah winters push costs toward the top of the range. Our [winter guide](/guides/hot-tub-utah-winter/) covers how to keep that in check.
- **How you use it.** Long, frequent soaks with the cover off cost more than a quick evening soak.

Budget a little for water care too: sanitizer, test strips, and filters. Bring a water sample to our showroom any time and we'll test it for free.

## 4. Lifetime cost

A quality hot tub typically lasts 15–20 years or more with proper care. Pumps, heaters and covers may need replacing along the way, and we repair every brand, not just the ones we sell. When you compare two spas, compare the **total cost over the years you'll own it**: purchase price + installation + monthly running costs + likely repairs. A better-insulated spa with a slightly higher price can cost less overall.

## A simple budgeting checklist

- Pick a size and tier (how many people, how often, what features)
- Decide between plug-and-play 120V and a 240V installation
- Get an electrician's quote if you need a new circuit
- Choose a base: existing patio, new concrete pad, or compacted gravel
- Measure delivery access from the street to the spot
- Add steps, a cover lifter, and water-care supplies
- Ask for an out-the-door quote, including Utah sales tax
