We carry two hot tub manufacturers, **Artesian Spas** and **Nordic Hot Tubs**, because they cover very different needs and budgets. This guide compares every line we sell so you can narrow things down before you visit. We're not going to tell you one brand is "the best." The right spa depends on your space, your electrical setup, how you'll use it, and your budget.

- **Artesian Spas** (Las Vegas, Nevada) makes [Island Spas](/spa-models/island-spas/), [Artesian Elite](/spa-models/artesian-elite/), [South Seas Spas](/spa-models/south-seas-spas/), [Garden Spas](/spa-models/garden-spas/), and [TidalFit](/spa-models/swim-spas/) swim spas and hot tubs.

- **Covana** makes [automated covers](/covana/) that rise into a gazebo-style enclosure. Ask us about fit for the spa you choose.

## Artesian Spas lines

### Artesian Elite: the top of the range

The Elite line is Artesian's luxury hydrotherapy spa. Standard features include the **DIRECTFLOW Personal Control System** (adjust the jets at your seat), 316L stainless steel H2O PowerFlow jets, dual footblasters, a 5.5 kW titanium heater, WiFi, DynaBrite Elite LED lighting, an ozone system, and full-foam insulation with a PermaBase ABS pan. Models run from the 4-person Quail Ridge to the 9-person Pelican Bay.

**Choose it if:** hydrotherapy is the main reason you're buying, and you want the most control over every seat.

### Island Spas: build it your way

Island Spas start with a size and seat layout. Then you pick a trim. **Essential** includes 32–44 Helix jets, two jet pumps plus a circulation pump, and full foam. **Luxury** adds more jets, a third pump, footblasters and an ozonator. **Elite** goes up to 72 Helix jets and adds DirectFlow personal control. There are eight models, up to the 9-seat Bimini.

**Choose it if:** you want a premium Artesian spa but would rather pick the feature level than pay for everything.

### South Seas Spas: variety at accessible prices

South Seas offers more than fifteen models in Deluxe and Standard classes, with lounger and bench layouts, pillowfall massagers and neck-and-shoulder jet systems. Most models use a 5.5 kW heater on 240V. The compact 4-seat **519P** is a 120V plug-in.

**Choose it if:** you want lots of shape and seating choices without stepping up to Island or Elite pricing.

### Garden Spas: compact plug-and-play

Nine compact models from 2 to 6 seats, designed for smaller homes and tight spaces. They plug into a standard 120V/15A outlet with the included GFCI cord. Grandwood cabinets are maintenance-free.

**Choose it if:** you live in a condo or townhome, have a small patio, or want to skip a 240V installation. Read [110V vs 240V](/guides/110v-vs-240v-hot-tubs/) first so you know what to expect from a smaller heater.

### TidalFit: swim spas and hot tubs

TidalFit exercise pools (Active EP-12, ActivePlus EP-12 and Pro EP-15) have a True Variable Speed swim current and are heated for year-round use. TidalFit also makes standalone hot tubs (DTL-6, DTL-8, DT-19 and DT-21). See [Swim Spa vs Pool in Utah](/guides/swim-spa-vs-pool-utah/).

**Choose it if:** you want to swim or exercise at home and a full pool doesn't fit your yard or your Utah swim season.

## Nordic Hot Tubs series

Nordic spas share a few traits: **Nordic Star stainless steel jets**, a textured, non-skid **HDPE Permashell** surface, Permawood or cedar cabinets, and Nordic's Dual Therapy System, which pairs high-volume whirlpool flow with targeted jets. Many models are 110V or 110V/220V convertible, which makes installation simpler.

- **Luxury Series:** the 6-person Encore LS, for example, has 53 jets, a 3 hp and a 2 hp pump, and the Northern Lighting Package standard. Rendezvous LS seats up to 8.
- **Sport Edition:** built around recovery, with jets aimed at the back, legs and feet. Models range from the 2-person D'Amour SE to the 6-person Encore and Jubilee SE.
- **Modern Series:** clean, contemporary shapes. The Bella MS is only 34.5 inches wide and fits through most standard doorways. Models are 110V/220V convertible.
- **Classic Series:** Nordic's signature round tubs with multi-level, barrier-free seating, from the Impulse Classic (Nordic's shallowest at 31 inches) to the Crown XL. Most are convertible.
- **ALL-IN-110V:** plug-and-play versions of popular models, with Nordic Wrap insulation and the Nordic Clean ozonator standard.

**Choose Nordic if:** you want a straightforward, durable spa at a value price, like round tubs, or want a plug-and-play or convertible option.

## How to compare spas yourself

1. **Sit in them.** Seat depth, lounger fit and jet placement feel different for every body. Our [Midvale showroom](/contact/) has models on display from all the brands we carry.
2. **Match the electrical to your home.** Plug-and-play, convertible, or 240V. See [110V vs 240V](/guides/110v-vs-240v-hot-tubs/).
3. **Ask about insulation** if you'll soak through Utah winters. See our [winter guide](/guides/hot-tub-utah-winter/).
4. **Compare total cost,** not just the sticker price: installation, running costs and accessories. See [what a hot tub costs in Utah](/guides/hot-tub-cost-utah/).
5. **Think about service.** We repair every brand, and we're a short drive away for anyone on the Wasatch Front.
