## Park City spas start in Midvale

Park City is about 33 miles from our showroom, roughly 45 minutes via I-80 and Parleys Canyon when roads are clear. It's part of our service area, and the drive is worth it to compare models side by side. Sitting in a spa is the only real way to know if it fits you.

## Planning for 7,000 feet

Central Park City sits at about 7,010 feet, more than 2,600 feet higher than our Midvale showroom. That changes what matters most:

**Cold and snow.** Expect longer, colder winters and much more snow than the Salt Lake Valley. Your spa will run its heater more, and its cover will carry snow for months.

- Choose strong insulation: full foam on [Island Spas](/spa-models/island-spas/), full foam with a PermaBase ABS pan on [Artesian Elite](/spa-models/artesian-elite/), or Nordic's Nordic Wrap upgrade.
- Pick a 240V spa. The 4–5.5 kW heaters on the 240V spas we carry recover heat many times faster than a 1 kW plug-and-play heater. See [110V vs 240V](/guides/110v-vs-240v-hot-tubs/).
- Get a heavy-duty, well-fitting [cover](/covers/) and brush heavy snow off regularly. A [Covana](/covana/) automated cover keeps closing up easy in deep winter.

**Placement and roof snow.** Keep the spa out of the path of snow and ice sliding off roofs, and plan a short, clear path from the door. Non-slip [steps](/steps/) are a must.

**Access.** Steep driveways, decks and tight mountain lots can complicate delivery. Measure carefully and plan the route before the spa is ordered, and avoid scheduling delivery during a storm cycle.

**Rules and approvals.** Many Park City neighborhoods have HOAs, and the historic district has its own design guidelines. Check before adding exterior structures or a gazebo-style enclosure.

## If your home is a rental

Hot tubs in short-term rentals see heavy use from many guests. Plan for more frequent water testing, filter cleaning and drain-and-refill cycles, and a locking cover. We stock filters, chemicals and covers at the showroom.
