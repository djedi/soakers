## Murray: right up the road

Murray is the closest city to our showroom. From central Murray it's about 3 miles straight down State Street to 6905 S State St in Midvale, around 6 minutes when traffic is light. That makes it easy to stop in for filters, chemicals or a free water test, and it's a short trip for our service calls.

## Things Murray homeowners should know

**Murray has its own power company.** Unlike most of the valley, most Murray homes buy electricity from **Murray City Power**, a publicly owned municipal utility the city has run since 1913. Your spa's monthly electric cost will follow their residential rates, so it's worth looking at their rate schedule when you compare models. In Utah, most modern hot tubs cost roughly $20–$50 a month to run. Good insulation and a tight cover keep you at the low end. See our [cost guide](/guides/hot-tub-cost-utah/).

**Established neighborhoods.** Many Murray neighborhoods are well established, with mature trees, fenced side yards and older electrical panels. Before ordering a full-size 240V spa, have an electrician check your panel capacity. If adding a circuit is impractical, a plug-and-play [Garden Spa](/spa-models/garden-spas/) or [Nordic ALL-IN-110V](/spa-models/nordic-all-in-110v/) model uses a standard outlet.

**Placement.** Mature trees are lovely but drop leaves and needles into a spa. Placing the tub out from under the canopy, with a good [cover](/covers/), cuts down on filter cleaning.
