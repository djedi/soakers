## South Jordan hot tubs

South Jordan is about 8 miles from our Midvale showroom, roughly 12 minutes in light traffic. Whether you're in one of the older neighborhoods east of Bangerter or in Daybreak, the first step is the same: come sit in a few spas and figure out what size and seating fit the way your family will use it.

## Living in Daybreak? Start with design review

Daybreak's community rules list **hot tubs among the exterior modifications that need Design Review Committee approval** before installation. The Daybreak Community Association's process includes an online application with supporting documents and a written decision. Plan for that time in your project. Things that help your application:

- The spa's dimensions and a site plan showing where it will sit
- Screening or landscaping, if you plan any
- Details on any structure over the spa, such as a pergola or a [Covana](/covana/) automated cover that rises into a gazebo-style enclosure

Ask us for the manufacturer spec sheet for the model you choose. Always check the current Daybreak Resident Design Guidelines for the exact requirements.

## Smaller lots, smart choices

Many South Jordan homes, especially in newer planned communities, have compact backyards. Good fits include:

- **[Garden Spas](/spa-models/garden-spas/):** 2 to 6 seats, plug into a standard 120V/15A outlet, no new circuit needed
- **[Nordic Modern Series](/spa-models/nordic-modern/):** contemporary shapes, including the 34.5-inch-wide Bella MS
- **[Nordic ALL-IN-110V](/spa-models/nordic-all-in-110v/):** plug-and-play with Nordic Wrap insulation standard

If you have room and want maximum hydrotherapy, look at [Island Spas](/spa-models/island-spas/) or the [Artesian Elite](/spa-models/artesian-elite/).

## Electrical and base

Full-size spas need a dedicated 240V circuit and a level, solid base. Plug-and-play spas skip the circuit but still need a level surface. Read [110V vs 240V](/guides/110v-vs-240v-hot-tubs/) and our [cost guide](/guides/hot-tub-cost-utah/) to budget the whole project.
