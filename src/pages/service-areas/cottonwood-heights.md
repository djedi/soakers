## Cottonwood Heights: closest to the canyons

Cottonwood Heights sits at the mouths of Big and Little Cottonwood Canyons, and its center is higher than any of the other valley cities we profile, at about 4,790 feet. It's also close: about 5 miles from our showroom, roughly 8 minutes west on Fort Union Blvd and then a short hop up State Street.

## Built for bench-level winters

Homes on the east bench tend to see colder nights and more snow than the valley floor. For a spa that gets used all winter, we'd focus on three things:

1. **Insulation.** [Island Spas](/spa-models/island-spas/) list full foam on every trim, [Artesian Elite](/spa-models/artesian-elite/) uses full foam with a PermaBase ABS pan, and Nordic offers Nordic Wrap, which the manufacturer says adds R-9.5.
2. **The cover.** A heavy, tight-fitting [cover](/covers/) with locking straps, and a lifter so you actually close it every time. A [Covana](/covana/) automated cover makes closing up on a cold night as easy as turning a key.
3. **Heater size.** A 240V spa with a 4–5.5 kW heater recovers far faster than a 1 kW plug-and-play heater. See [110V vs 240V](/guides/110v-vs-240v-hot-tubs/).

Our [Utah winter guide](/guides/hot-tub-utah-winter/) covers snow on the cover, freeze protection and safe footing.

## Après-ski and recovery

With two of Utah's big ski canyons on the doorstep, a lot of soaks here start after a day on the mountain. For recovery-focused hydrotherapy:

- **[Nordic Sport Edition](/spa-models/nordic-sport/):** designed for people who push their bodies hard, with jets aimed at the back, legs and feet
- **[Artesian Elite](/spa-models/artesian-elite/):** DIRECTFLOW Personal Control lets each person adjust jet intensity at their seat
- **[Hydrotherapy jets](/hydrotherapy-jets/):** compare jet types before you choose

## Practical notes for Cottonwood Heights homes

- **Snow access.** Plan a short, clear path from the door to the spa. Non-slip [steps](/steps/) and a spot for ice melt away from the cabinet help.
- **Sloped lots.** Bench lots often slope, so get the base level and solid before delivery.
- **Electrical.** Most full-size spas need a dedicated 240V circuit. Budget for it early with our [cost guide](/guides/hot-tub-cost-utah/).
